# User Guide

Handcrafted Haven is a marketplace for shoppers and sellers. The app supports browsing products, creating accounts, managing a cart, checking out, leaving reviews, and managing a seller profile from the dashboard.

## For shoppers
1. Open the homepage or the marketplace page to browse products.
2. Open a product detail page to see the description, stock, seller info, and reviews.
3. Add a product to the cart, then review quantities on the cart page.
4. Continue to checkout, enter shipping details, and place the order.
5. Use the payment page to complete the payment handoff and review the success screen.

## For account management
1. Create an account from the register page.
2. Sign in to access protected areas such as the cart, orders, and dashboard.
3. Log out from the navigation when you are finished.

## For sellers
1. Open the dashboard after signing in.
2. Create a seller profile before adding products.
3. Create, edit, and delete products from the dashboard.
4. Use the inventory page to update stock levels and monitor low-stock items.

## For reviews
1. Open a product page while signed in.
2. Leave a star rating and comment.
3. Edit or delete your own review later from the same product page.

## Common states you will see
- Loading screens while data is being fetched.
- Empty states when a cart, order list, or review list has no items yet.
- Error banners when auth, data loading, or server actions fail.
